<p align="center">
  <b>EaglerCraft: EaglerCraft Archives!</b><br>
  An Eaglercraft website inspired by the iconic Minecraft website of 2013, bringing back the classic design, atmosphere, and nostalgia of Minecraft's golden era!

  Explore an extensive, carefully organized archive of Eaglercraft Client Versions, all brought together in one convenient place. Discover the origins of Eaglercraft, its early beginnings, development, evolution, major releases, different client versions, and the community that helped shape its legacy.

  Revisit classic releases, explore the history behind each version, discover forgotten builds, and learn how Eaglercraft evolved over time. From its earliest beginnings to its most recognizable releases, this project aims to preserve and celebrate the history of Eaglercraft.

  Whether you're a longtime player, a returning member of the community, or simply curious about Eaglercraft, there's something here to explore. Everything Eaglercraft, all in one place!
</p>
